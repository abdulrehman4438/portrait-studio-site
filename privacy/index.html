Operator Notice & Legal Review

This policy applies to the iOS application currently operating under the provisional title Portrait Studio (internal identifier: RetroPortrait), operated by REPLACE_BEFORE_PUBLISHING_OPERATOR_LEGAL_NAME (“we”, “our”, or “us”). Before public commercial distribution, a qualified legal practitioner should review these disclosures for compliance with your target jurisdictions.

1. Overview & Privacy Principles

Portrait Studio is designed to provide creative, nostalgic 1980s-style portrait transformations with strict data privacy controls. Our core principles are:

  • No cross-app tracking: We do not track you across third-party apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal data.
  • Zero advertising SDKs: There are no third-party advertisements, behavioral ad trackers, or marketing pixels in our application.
  • Explicit consent for upload: Selecting a photo in your device picker does not upload it. Photos are only transmitted to our private backend when you explicitly confirm and initiate a portrait generation.
  • Private storage: Your uploaded photos and generated portraits are stored in private cloud storage and are never made public.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the service, manage user accounts, deliver AI generations, and process purchases.

A. Account and Authentication Information

When you sign in using Sign in with Apple, we receive:

  • A unique, persistent Apple-generated user identifier that links your device to your Portrait Studio account.
  • Your email address, or an Apple Private Relay email address if you choose to conceal your personal email.
  • Session authentication tokens, stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device.

B. User-Submitted Photos

When you explicitly request a portrait generation, the application uploads:

  • The specific image you selected from your photo library or camera.
  • Your selected artistic style identifier (for example, 1980s Yearbook, Glamour, Rockstar, Studio, or Street).
  • Framing and crop parameters configured by you inside the app.

C. Financial and Purchase Information

All subscription and credit pack purchases are processed directly by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details. We receive and store:

  • Anonymous transaction identifiers and receipt tokens confirming valid payment.
  • Subscription lifecycle events (such as purchase, renewal, cancellation, or refund).
  • Your account credit balance ledger, ensuring purchased and introductory credits are accurately tracked.

D. Technical and Diagnostic Logs

To maintain system security, monitor queue health, and prevent fraudulent abuse, our servers generate limited technical logs. These logs include IP addresses, request timestamps, API route endpoints, HTTP status codes, and error messages. Technical logs do not contain user portrait image contents.

3. Service Providers & Infrastructure

To deliver our service securely, we rely on established infrastructure providers who act as data processors under strict confidentiality obligations:

Service Provider Purpose / Role Data Handled
Apple Inc. App distribution, Sign in with Apple, payment processing via StoreKit. Apple ID authentication tokens, purchase receipts, billing data (handled exclusively by Apple).
Cloudflare, Inc. API hosting (Workers), background queues, image processing, and private temporary storage (R2). Encrypted API payloads, ephemeral queued generation jobs, watermarked previews, and temporary private files.
Supabase, Inc. User account database and credit balance ledger. User identifier, email (or relay email), credit ledger, and job status records.
OpenAI, L.L.C. AI model processing for style transformation. The uploaded user photo and server-defined styling instructions for portrait rendering.
RevenueCat, Inc. In-app purchase validation and subscription lifecycle management. Anonymous App Store purchase transaction receipts and subscription status events.

4. Data Retention & Automatic Cleanup

We do not retain your portrait photos on cloud servers longer than necessary to deliver and verify them:

  • Unpaid Watermarked Generations: When a watermarked preview is created, the clean cloud version is retained for approximately 12 hours. If not unlocked with a credit within this window, the clean file is permanently purged from cloud storage.
  • Unlocked / Paid Clean Generations: Once a portrait is unlocked with a credit (or generated with a paid credit), the clean cloud copy is retained for up to approximately 48 hours to guarantee reliable local download and recovery on your device.
  • Device Storage: Portraits saved to the protected local album within the Portrait Studio app remain on your iPhone until you delete them or uninstall the app. Images explicitly exported to your system Photos app are governed by your device storage.
  • Account Records: Your user identifier and credit ledger remain active while your account exists so you can access unused credits across app launches.

5. Your Privacy Choices & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Photo Deletion: You can delete any generated portrait directly in the app’s “My Photos” album. This instantly removes the local copy and invalidates cloud references.
  • Complete Account Deletion: Inside the iOS app, navigate to Account Settings > Delete Account. Initiating deletion permanently removes your account record, authentication mapping, and any associated cloud files once active generation finishes. (Narrow anti-fraud transaction records required by law may be retained).
  • Subscription Notice: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store subscription. Because subscriptions are managed by Apple, you must cancel them via your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
  • Photo Consent Revocation: You can revoke photo consent at any time prior to generation by cancelling the photo picker or removing the selected photo.

For detailed step-by-step guidance, visit our dedicated Privacy Choices page.

6. Children’s Privacy

Portrait Studio is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact our support team immediately so we can remove the data.
