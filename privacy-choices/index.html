Your Privacy Choices

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 Last Updated: October 1, 2026

You maintain direct control over your photographs, account records, and consents within Portrait Studio.

1. Revoking Photo Consent & Pre-Upload Selection

Selecting a photo in the iOS photo picker does not transmit the image to our servers. Your selected image remains exclusively on your device until you press the Create photo button.

How to revoke consent before upload:

  • Tap Replace to choose a different photo.
  • Navigate back or close the composer to abandon the selection. No data or image will be transmitted to our cloud service.

2. Deleting Generated Portraits

You can delete any generated portrait from your account at any time:

  1. Open the Portrait Studio app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the My Photos tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select the portrait you wish to remove.
  4. Tap the Trash / Delete button and confirm.

This action immediately deletes the local copy from your app's protected storage and invalidates server references to the image.

3. Complete Account Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your entire account and associated records:

  1. Open the Portrait Studio app.
  2. Tap the Account icon in the top right corner.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted.

What happens during account deletion:

  • Any active, in-flight generation is allowed to finalize so server resources do not leak.
  • Your Apple authentication mapping, email address (or private relay address), and account record in our database are permanently deleted.
  • All associated portrait files in our private cloud storage (R2) are immediately purged.
  • Your local session credentials stored in the iOS Keychain are removed.
Important Subscription Notice

Apple does not allow third-party developers to cancel subscriptions on your behalf. Deleting your account inside the app does not cancel an active Apple subscription. Before or immediately after deleting your account, open your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions and cancel any active Portrait Studio subscription to prevent future billing by Apple.
