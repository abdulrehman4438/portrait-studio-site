Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Find concise answers for authentication, credit synchronization, queue delays, and managing your Apple subscription.

Sign in with Apple & Accounts

How do I sign in? Portrait Studio exclusively uses Sign in with Apple for private authentication. Tap the account button in the top navigation bar and select Sign in with Apple. You can use your Apple ID email or choose "Hide My Email" (Apple Private Relay).

I changed devices. How do I get my account back? Simply sign in using the exact same Apple ID on your new iPhone. Your credit ledger is securely linked to your Apple user identifier and will synchronize automatically.

Generation Times & Delays

How long does a portrait take to generate? Most transformations take between 20 and 45 seconds. During peak hours or high queue volume, cloud processing can occasionally take up to 90 seconds.

Can I leave the app while it processes? Yes. Portrait Studio tracks generation jobs via our cloud queue. If you close the app or lose connection, your job continues in the background. When you reopen the app, it checks the server and restores your completed preview.

Failed Jobs & Credit Protection

What happens if a generation fails due to a network glitch? If an image cannot be processed or a technical error occurs, your credit is not charged. The system performs an automatic server-side refund, returning your credit to your available balance.

Saved-Request Recovery

What if my phone died or restarted during creation? Portrait Studio automatically preserves your in-flight request ID and local draft. When the app launches, it checks the cloud status with a lightweight check. If the job finished, it displays your preview without asking you to re-upload or spend another credit.

Photo Credits & Carryover Rules

Do purchased credits expire at the end of the month? No. Unused purchased credits (from monthly subscriptions or top-up packs) carry over indefinitely while the service is operational.

How many credits does an unlock cost? Exactly one credit. One credit unlocks the exact watermarked preview you reviewed without altering the image.

Restoring Purchases & Syncing Credits

My purchase completed in the App Store but my credit count didn't update. What should I do?

  1. Ensure your iPhone is connected to an active Wi-Fi or cellular data connection.
  2. Open Portrait Studio and tap the Account icon in the top right.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases / Check Balance. This forces a secure sync between Apple StoreKit, RevenueCat, and our database ledger.

Managing or Cancelling Subscriptions

How do I cancel my monthly subscription? Subscriptions are billed and managed directly through Apple. To cancel or modify your plan:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your Apple ID name at the very top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Portrait Studio.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

Will I lose my credits if I cancel? No. Cancelling prevents future monthly billing, but all previously granted, unused credits remain available in your account.

Saving to the Photos App & Expiry Notice

How do I save clean portraits to my iPhone camera roll? Once you unlock a portrait with a credit, tap Save to Photos. If prompted, grant Portrait Studio permission to save images to your library.

Important cloud deadline: Clean cloud copies are retained on our servers for up to approximately 48 hours following an unlock for recovery purposes. We strongly recommend tapping "Save to Photos" immediately so a permanent copy is safely stored in your camera roll.

Deleting Photos & Account Deletion

How do I delete individual portraits? Open the My Photos tab in the app, select any portrait, and tap the trash icon.

How do I completely delete my account? Go to Account Settings > Delete Account inside the app. For detailed options, read our Privacy Choices page.
