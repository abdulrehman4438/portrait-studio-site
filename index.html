AUTHENTIC LAB PIPELINE

How Portrait Studio Works

Every portrait follows a predictable, private pipeline built strictly for iPhone users. We prioritize privacy, exact output delivery, and zero unexpected billings.

STEP 01 SIGN-IN

Sign in with Apple

Authenticate securely using Apple's private credentials. Your session tokens are stored in the device Keychain, and you can hide your personal email address.

STEP 02 CONSENT

Select Your Photo

Choose a solo or couple photo from your library. Your photo is never uploaded until you explicitly tap Create Photo to start generation.

STEP 03 PROCESSING

Private Generation

Our private Cloudflare and OpenAI pipeline transforms your photo using curated 1980s aesthetic prompts into a nostalgic era portrait.

STEP 04 EXACT UNLOCK

Exact Watermark Removal

Review your watermarked preview. When you spend a credit, the exact same stored image is unlocked cleanly—never regenerated or altered.

PRIVACY • DATA LIFECYCLE

Private by Design

Your portraits belong to you. We do not maintain public galleries, sell data, or use your photos for advertising.

Automated Cloud Retention Windows

To protect your privacy while allowing sufficient time for viewing and download, our cloud infrastructure enforces strict deletion windows:

  • Unpaid Previews: Unpaid successful cloud generations remain available for approximately 12 hours, after which cloud originals are permanently purged.
  • Unlocked / Paid Portraits: After an unlock or paid generation, the clean cloud copy is retained for up to approximately 48 hours to allow reliable local download and recovery.
  • Device Album Storage: Downloaded portraits saved to your iPhone's protected local album remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app.
  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual portrait anytime from the "My Photos" screen, or trigger complete account deletion in App Settings.
